# Dmitrii Russu

Computer science and business student in Zurich. I like building products where code and design meet, from the first sketch to something people can download and use.

## What I'm building

**[Handle](https://github.com/dirussu/handle)** is a macOS assistant that lives in the notch. It sees the screen, points at and clicks things, works with files, calendar and reminders, runs automations on a schedule, and delegates bigger jobs to its own sub-agents. You bring your own AI key, and nothing of yours is stored off your Mac. Built with Swift and SwiftUI, open source, with a [downloadable build](https://github.com/dirussu/handle/releases/latest).

**[mappy](https://meetmappy.com)** is a Mac app for turning messy thoughts into mind maps. I built it because I needed it myself.
